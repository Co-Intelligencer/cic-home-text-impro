Before you gather

Feel ready for
your first circle.

01How do my guests join?+

Host with an account. Guests join free in their browser using a link or six-digit room code.

02How do we choose when AI participates?+

Begin in Human Mode, then invite Milo or live translation when your group wants to use them. The room shows which features are active.

03How is our conversation handled?+

Video and audio are encrypted in transit and relayed live through Cloudflare's realtime network. You choose when recording starts. Saved recordings are automatically deleted after a period of time to reduce server costs. You can delete them sooner. An invited AI feature processes the audio it needs while active.

04How does Milo take part?+

Milo joins by voice and listens to the conversation in real time. It speaks when the talking stick reaches its seat. You can invite it to join or ask it to leave during the circle.

05What does beta mean for live translation?+

You can use live translation today with credits. Quality varies by language pair, and availability may change as we improve it. You pay for active minutes. If we pause translation, your circle continues in the original voices.

06How do we listen across languages?+

Each person chooses their listening language. They hear a translated voice with the original softly present underneath. Translation uses 2 credits per listener per active minute and is currently in beta.

07What happens when our credits run out?+

Your circle continues in Human Mode. Milo leaves and translation pauses while your group keeps talking. You choose whether to purchase more credits.

08Can I use my own branding?+

Your room carries your circle's name. Custom logos and visual identity are in preparation. Talk to us about what your practice or program needs.
