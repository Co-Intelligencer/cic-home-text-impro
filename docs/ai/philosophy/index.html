Philosophy

Make room for deep listening.

Human connection shapes the circle. Invite AI to take part in real time when its contribution serves the conversation your group wants to have.

The practice

A practice you share.

Talking-piece practices appear in council work, Quaker meetings, restorative justice, and supervision groups. People sit where they can see each other, an object marks the speaking turn, and the group agrees to listen.

Co-Intelligence Circle brings that practice online. Your group chooses a speaking order and passes a visible talking stick. How you listen to each other gives the structure its meaning.

Bring an old practice into your next conversation.

What listening can make possible

Hear what each person brings.

Each person brings experience, intuition, and knowledge. A full speaking turn gives those perspectives time to meet the group's collective intelligence. Through deep listening and trust, make room for higher wisdom to emerge.

Coherence takes practice. The shared understanding you develop can save time in later collaboration and help your group work with greater trust and peace of mind.

The center

Gather around a shared center.

The shared center gives your group a common point of attention. Choose what belongs there for your circle and let it support the conversation's purpose.

AI in the circle

Invite AI into the conversation.

Milo listens alongside your group in real time and contributes when the talking stick reaches its seat, or when its name is called in an open round.

Your group chooses when Milo joins. Its published instructions describe its name, voice participation, and speaking language. When credits run out, Milo leaves while the human conversation continues.

Privacy

Know how your conversation is handled.

Video and audio are encrypted in transit and relayed live through Cloudflare's realtime network. The host chooses when recording starts. Saved recordings are automatically deleted after a period of time to reduce server costs. You can export them or delete them sooner. An invited AI feature processes the audio it needs while active.
