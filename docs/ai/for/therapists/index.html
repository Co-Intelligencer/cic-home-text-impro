For therapists & group practices

Give each person time to finish.

A talking-stick video room for structured group conversations. Decide whether Milo takes part in real time, and make those choices visible to your group.

What the room does for the work

Structure for attentive group work.

·

See who has the speaking turn

The visible talking stick shows who is speaking. Use the speaking order to help your group listen and take turns.

·

Make room for a full thought

Give each speaker time to finish, with an optional timer to guide the pace. Leave room for silence when the group needs it.

·

Guests join in their browser

Guests join free in their browser with a link or six-digit room code. Hosting requires an account.

·

Choose when recording starts

The host starts recording, and the room shows when it is active. Enabled AI features process audio while the group can see they are running.

Preparing the room

Set up the room for your group.

01

Keep one room per group

Paid plans include named rooms that keep their links. Use a separate room for each recurring group.

02

Choose the tools your practice needs

Starter supports human circles. Other plans offer AI participation and transcription. Review each plan and the privacy details for your practice.

03

Hold the session

Set the speaking order, optional timer, and shared center before the group arrives. During the session, use the talking stick to guide the turns.

The questions worth asking

Practical questions for your practice.

01How is transcription controlled?+

Starter disables transcription at plan level. On plans that include transcription, it begins when enabled, and the room shows when it is active.

02Is a session recorded?+

On a plan that includes recording, the host chooses when it starts. Saved recordings are automatically deleted after a period of time to reduce server costs. You can export them or delete them sooner.

03Where does the video go?+

Video and audio are encrypted in transit and relayed live through Cloudflare's realtime network. The host chooses when recording starts.

04Do my clients need an account?+

Guests join free in their browser with a link or six-digit room code. Hosting requires an account.

05Is this a medical device or a certified system?+

Co-Intelligence Circle is a talking-stick video room. It is not a medical device or certified clinical system. You are responsible for assessing its clinical and legal suitability for your work.
