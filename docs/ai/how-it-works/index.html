How it works

Bring people and AI into
one conversation.

Gather your people and invite Milo to take part in the live conversation. A visible talking stick helps the group follow each turn, and live translation lets listeners choose a language.

The method

Give each person a full turn.

The person holding the talking stick has the speaking turn. Choose clockwise or counterclockwise rounds, or an open round for free conversation. An optional timer helps your group keep the pace you agree on. Taking turns gives quieter voices room and makes listening part of the practice.

Following the speaking turn

Follow the conversation together.

The room shows who holds the stick and who comes next. A shared speaking order helps your group give each person time and attention.

A shared speaking order
Everyone follows the same room state, including who holds the stick and where it can pass next.
You control your microphone
Passing the stick mutes your microphone by default. You can unmute yourself when you need to speak; the talking stick supports an agreement to take turns.
When someone disconnects
If the speaker disconnects, the stick returns to the shared center after fifteen seconds. Switching browser tabs preserves the turn.
Live video and recording
Video and audio are encrypted in transit and relayed live through Cloudflare's realtime network. The host chooses when recording starts.

Invite another perspective.

01 / Milo, the AI participantInvited
Illustration placeholder

An AI participant in real time.

Milo listens alongside your group in real time and contributes when the talking stick reaches its seat, or when its name is called in an open round.

Milo uses 1 credit per active minute, when it speaks or holds the stick. A single purchased credit costs €0.10. When credits run out, Milo leaves and your group continues talking.

02 / Live translationBeta. Live translation is available today. Quality varies by language pair, and availability may change during improvements. If translation pauses, your circle continues in the original voices.Per listener
Illustration placeholder

Listen across languages.

Each listener chooses a language. They hear a translated voice with the speaker's original voice softly present underneath. Translation quality varies by language pair.

Live translation uses 2 credits per listener per active minute. Each listener chooses when to enable it, and the room shows a usage counter.

Prepare your first circle.

01

Create a room

Name your room and set an optional timer. In the circle settings, you can adjust Milo's role to suit your conversation.

02

Share one link

Guests join free in their browser with a link or six-digit room code. Hosting requires an account.

03

Pass the stick

Give each person a full turn. Invite Milo to join when your group wants another perspective.
