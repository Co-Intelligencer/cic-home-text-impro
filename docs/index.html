Before you gather

Feel ready for
your first circle.

01How do my guests join?+

Share a link or a six-digit room code. Guests enter directly in their browser and join free. You need an account to host.

02Can I hold a circle entirely in Human Mode?+

Yes. Human Mode includes the talking stick, circle flow, and optional turn timer. You choose whether to invite Milo or use live translation.

03How is our conversation handled?+

Video and audio are encrypted in transit and forwarded live through Cloudflare's realtime network. The host chooses when recording begins. Recordings are kept for seven days and can be deleted sooner. When you invite an AI feature, the audio it needs is processed while that feature is active. The platform is built in Germany under European privacy standards.

04How does Milo take part?+

Milo begins listening when you invite it. You write its role, which guides its participation. A short, published instruction also sets its name, its audio-only participation, and its speaking language. Milo speaks when the talking stick reaches its seat. Your group can see its role and when it is active.

05What does beta mean for live translation?+

Live translation works today, and you can buy credits to use it. Quality varies by language pair, and the feature may change or briefly pause during improvements. You pay for the minutes it runs. Your circle continues in the original voices if translation pauses.

06How do we listen across languages?+

Each person chooses their listening language. They hear a translated voice with the original softly present underneath. Translation is billed per listening participant and active minute, and is currently in beta.

07What happens when our credits run out?+

Your circle continues in Human Mode. Milo leaves and translation pauses, while your group keeps talking. You choose whether to buy more credits; additional charges require your action.

08Can I use my own branding?+

Your room carries your circle's name. Custom logos and visual identity are in preparation. Talk to us about what your practice or program needs.
