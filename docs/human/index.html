Before you gather

Feel ready for
your first circle.

01How do my guests join?+

Host with an account. Guests join free in their browser using a link or six-digit room code.

02How do speaking turns work?+

The person holding the talking stick speaks while everyone else listens. Choose clockwise or counterclockwise turns, or an open round where the group chooses who speaks next.

03How do I prepare a circle?+

Name your room, choose a speaking order, and set an optional turn timer. Prepare a shared center, then send your invitation link.

04How is our conversation handled?+

Video and audio are encrypted in transit and relayed live through Cloudflare's realtime network. The host chooses when recording starts. Saved recordings are automatically deleted after a period of time to reduce server costs. You can delete them sooner.

05How many people can I invite?+

Free includes up to six people, Starter eight, and Basic or Pro twelve. These limits include you as host. Contact us about Studio.

06How long can we meet?+

Free circles last up to 30 minutes each. Starter, Basic and Pro allow circles of any length within your monthly participant-hours. Participant-hours add up the time each person spends in all your circles.

07How do I cancel my plan?+

Cancel anytime. Your plan continues until the end of the period you paid for. Export any recordings you want to keep before your plan ends.

08Can I use my own branding?+

Your room carries your circle's name. Custom logos and visual identity are in preparation. Talk to us about what your practice or program needs.
