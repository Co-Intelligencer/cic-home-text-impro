A session, start to finish

Prepare your next session.

01

Prepare the room

Name your room, choose clockwise, counterclockwise, or open turns, and set an optional turn timer.

02

Share a link or room code

Clients can open the invitation link or enter a six-digit room code in their browser.

03

Stay with the conversation

The visible talking stick shows who has the turn. You can give your attention to the speaker and guide the group.

Questions, answered

The practical details.

01Can I keep the same room for a group that meets regularly?+

Paid plans include named rooms that keep their links. Share the same room link with your recurring group.

02How do I introduce the circle to a new participant?+

Show the group who holds the talking stick and how to pass it. Use the first round to practice taking turns.

03Do I need to tell my clients anything about AI?+

The host chooses when recording starts. Your group can see when recording is active.

04Can I run two groups at once?+

Some paid plans support parallel rooms. Contact us to confirm the number of simultaneous circles your practice needs.
