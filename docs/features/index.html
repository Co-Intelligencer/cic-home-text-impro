Features

Tools for your circle.

See which tools are available, which plans include them, and what is still in development.

The circle

  • Talking stick

    A visible talking stick shows who has the speaking turn.

    AvailableEvery plan, including free
  • Circle round & open round

    Follow a speaking order or open the round for free conversation.

    AvailableEvery plan, including free
  • Sunwise & earthwise

    Choose clockwise or counterclockwise turns.

    AvailableEvery plan, including free
  • Raised hands

    Raise a hand to ask for a turn. The speaker can pass the stick to a raised hand.

    AvailableEvery plan, including free
  • Turn timer

    Use a visible timer to guide the pace of each turn.

    AvailableEvery plan, including free
  • Guests join free

    Guests join free in their browser with a link or six-digit room code. Hosting requires an account.

    AvailableEvery plan, including free

In the room

  • Video & audio

    See your group around a shared center.

    AvailableEvery plan, including free
  • Screen sharing

    Share a screen into the circle.

    AvailableEvery plan, including free
  • Named rooms

    Keep a named room and its invitation link for a recurring group.

    AvailablePaid plans
  • Scheduling

    Plan a circle, find a time together, and invite your group.

    AvailablePaid plans

After the circle

  • Recording

    The host chooses when recording starts. Saved recordings are automatically deleted after a period of time to reduce server costs. You can export them or delete them sooner.

    AvailablePaid plans

For teams and practices

  • Parallel rooms

    Run more than one circle at the same time.

    AvailableContact us for plan availability
  • Facilitator seats

    Several facilitators on one account, with one shared balance.

    AvailableStudio
  • Team usage dashboard

    See how the team is using its plan.

    Coming soonStudio
  • White label

    Add your room name, logo, and visual identity. This feature is in development.

    Coming soonStudio
