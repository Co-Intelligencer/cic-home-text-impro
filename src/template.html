Questions, answered

The quiet details.

01Do guests need an account?+

No. Guests join with one link or a six-digit room code. Only the facilitator needs an account.

02Can the platform be white-labelled?+

Not yet. Rooms carry your circle's name today, but your own logo and visual identity are still in preparation. If that is what a wider program needs, talk to us and we will tell you honestly where it stands.

03Do I need to install anything?+

No. The circle runs in the browser — yours and your guests'. There is no app, no plugin and no download, on any device.

04How many people fit in a circle?+

Six on Free, eight on Starter, and twelve on Basic or Pro. Studio capacity is scoped with your team. Everyone stays in view around one shared centre, with the turn visible on every seat.

05Is our conversation private?+

Video and audio are encrypted in transit and relayed through Cloudflare's realtime network, which forwards the streams rather than storing them. A session is only recorded when the host starts a recording; those files are kept for seven days and can be deleted sooner. The platform is built in Germany under European privacy standards.

06What if someone has never been in a circle before?+

That is the normal case, and the room is built for it. The stick is visible, the turn order is visible, and the timer is visible — so nobody has to be told the rules twice. Most groups need one round to settle in.

07Can I cancel, and what happens to my rooms?+

Cancel anytime; the plan runs to the end of the period you paid for. Your recordings and transcripts stay available for their normal seven days, and you can export them before the plan ends.
