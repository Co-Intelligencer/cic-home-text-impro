Questions, answered

The quiet details.

01Do credits expire?+

Plan credits reset each month. Purchased credits never expire and are used only after your plan credits.

02Do my participants pay anything?+

No. Guests join via link or room code, without an account, on every plan — including translation when you enable it for them.

03What happens when my credits reach zero?+

The circle simply continues. Milo bows out and translation pauses — your meeting is never interrupted, and nothing is charged automatically.

04Can I change plans mid-month?+

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and unused credits carry into the new plan's month; downgrades apply at the next renewal.
