For facilitators & coaches

Hold the room, not the tech.

A video room built on the talking-stick method, for people whose work is holding a group. Your clients join free, with one link and no account.

What you can promise your group

Four things you can promise.

·

Your clients never pay

Guests join with one link, on every plan, including the free one. No account, no seat, no invoice with their name on it.

·

Nothing to install

It opens in a browser. No download, no plugin, and no ten minutes lost at the start of a session.

·

Nothing listens unless you invite it

Recording, transcription and any AI participant start off. The room shows everyone when one is on.

·

A plan where nothing listens at all

For supervision and other rooms where nothing may leave, there is a tier with no AI, no transcript and no exception.

A session, start to finish

Three steps, and the room is open.

01

Open the circle before they arrive

Name it, choose sunwise, earthwise or open flow, set a turn timer if the group needs one. Everything you would otherwise explain out loud is already decided.

02

Send one link, or read six digits

Your clients open it and they are in. If somebody is on a phone with no email, the six-digit code works just as well.

03

Hold the room, not the tech

The stick is visible, the turn is obvious, and nobody has to be told the rules twice. There is almost nothing left to operate once it starts.

If a circle asks for more

Invited, never assumed.

Milo takes a seat with a role you write yourself and speaks only when the stick reaches it. Live translation is chosen per listener. Both are off until you turn them on, both are visible to the room while they run, and turning them off leaves the circle exactly as it was.

Questions, answered

The practical details.

01Can I keep the same room for a group that meets regularly?+

Yes, on the paid plans. A named room keeps its link, so a group that meets monthly always arrives at the same address.

02What if a client has never sat in a circle before?+

The room explains itself: one person visibly holds the stick and everyone can see whose turn it is. In practice the first round is the explanation.

03Do I need to tell my clients anything about AI?+

Only if you invite it. Nothing transcribes, records or listens until you turn it on, and while it is on the room can see that it is. There is also a plan where none of it can be switched on at all.

04Can I run two groups at once?+

From the Practitioner plan upwards, yes — parallel rooms are what that tier is for. Below it you host one circle at a time.
